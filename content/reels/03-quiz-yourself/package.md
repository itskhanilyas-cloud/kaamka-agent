## 1. Topic and audience
Stop re-reading notes and make AI quiz you instead. A classic memory experiment shows why; ChatGPT's Study mode
and Gemini's Guided Learning make it easy, and both are free.
Audience: Indian college students heading into Nov–Dec semester exams; secondary: anyone preparing for interviews or certifications.

## 2. Why this idea is worth testing
Exam season is starting, re-reading is something nearly every student does, and the Reel gives one surprising,
well-sourced number (40% vs 61%) plus a copy-paste prompt. High save potential, and it tests a "myth-busting
with data" format against Reel 01's "offer + catch" format.

## 3. Research and verified claims
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Classic 2006 experiment: re-reading vs self-testing; final tests after 5 min, 2 days, 1 week | Roediger & Karpicke (2006), *Psychological Science* 17(3), 249–255 ([WashU record](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/test-enhanced-learning-taking-memory-tests-improves-long-term-ret/)) |
| One week later: repeated-testing group recalled 61%, repeated-study group 40%; studiers read the material ~14.2 times vs ~3.4 times | [APS Observer: Test-enhanced learning](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/test-enhanced-learning) |
| Re-reading wins at 5 minutes, loses later; studiers felt more confident | same two sources |
| ChatGPT Study mode: launched 29 Jul 2025 for logged-in Free, Plus, Pro and Team users; on from the tools menu; asks questions, hints, quizzes | [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!technology/openai-rolls-out-study-mode-to-encourage-active-learning-enn25073006432) |
| Gemini Guided Learning: rolled out Aug 2025, free for all Gemini users; step-by-step questions, flashcards | [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gemini-gets-a-guided-learning-mode-for-students-how-to-use-it/amp/) |

Wording kept careful: "about 14 times" vs "about 3 times, then tested themselves" (the paper's averages), and the
result is described as what was remembered after a week, not a guarantee of marks.
**Before publishing:** open ChatGPT and Gemini once and confirm the mode names still match the current apps.
