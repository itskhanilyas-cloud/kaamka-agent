# Reel 01: "Google ka AI plan, 1 saal FREE. Par ek catch hai."

Status: **rendered, not published** (2026-10-15). Video: 1080×1920, 29 s, H.264, silent audio track. Render: `python3 render.py out.mp4` in this folder.

## 1. Topic and audience
Google AI Plus is free for 12 months for eligible college students in India (claim by 31 Dec 2026), and it auto-renews at ₹399/month afterwards.
Audience: Indian college students (18–24) who use or want AI tools for study. Secondary: early-career professionals forwarding to younger siblings/friends.

## 2. Why this idea is worth testing
Timely (offer open until 31 Dec 2026), practical (3-step claim), and highly shareable ("send to a friend"). The "catch" (auto-renew) is the original angle: most posts only hype "free", so we earn trust by saying what to watch out for.
